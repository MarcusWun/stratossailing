Sailing on the Hudson

Sailing from the Hudson River into the Upper New York Bay offers a thrilling contrast: the peaceful, crisp river breeze against your face juxtaposed with awe-inspiring, up-close views of the towering Manhattan skyline, the majestic Statue of Liberty, and bustling maritime history all around you.
