Offshore 160

The Offshore 160 is a biennial, singlehanded and short-handed overnight sailing race hosted by the Newport Yacht Club in Rhode Island. It covers roughly 160 nautical miles in Rhode Island Sound and Block Island Sound, acting as a key qualifier for the grueling Bermuda One-Two race.
